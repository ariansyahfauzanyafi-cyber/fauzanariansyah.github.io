TraumaLog

Digital ransformation of "Trauma Room Registration" (From Paper to Digital)

UX Research | Wireframes | Prototyping | UI Design

This project was developed in collaboration with Städtisches Klinikum Solingen to transform the critical emergency registration process from a manual, paper-based form into a real-time digital ecosystem. The application utilizes a unique QR code for each patient, making it effortless for paramedics to scan and instantly access or update vital patient records on the go. Designed as a continuous digital journey, the form is not only initiated by paramedics in the ambulance during transport but is also updated later inside the hospital during active treatment. This seamless workflow also solves a major administrative bottleneck: at the end of every year, medical assistants must painstakingly review and fill out all missing patient information, a process that is now automated and streamlined through our digital database.

The 5-Step UX Process

  1. Discovery & Research

    Expert Briefing: Kickstarted the project with a briefing from a clinical doctor assistant at Städtisches Klinikum Solingen, analyzing the current paper-based protocol and ER procedures.

    Form Analysis: Analyzed the physical form to identify critical data requirements and prepared targeted questions to uncover hidden friction points.

  2. User Interviews & Synthesis

    User Interviews:Conducted, transcribed, and summarized expert interviews with medical professionals to define their exact needs under high-stress scenarios.

    User Personas: Developed target user personas to ensure the interface directly accommodates the cognitive load and physical limitations of active ER staff and paramedics.

  3. Workflow Mapping & Feature Planning

    Process Diagram (Prozessdiagramm):Mapped the step-by-step communication loop from the initial ambulance dispatch, through transit data-sharing, to the hospital's trauma room preparation.

    Implementation Matrix (Umsetzungstabelle): Structured a technical requirement sheet to map out exactly where and how critical data points (such as vitals and injury types) would live in the UI.

  4. Branding & Visual Identity

    Design System & CI: Established a custom corporate identity for the clinical app—designing a cohesive logo, an accessible high-contrast color palette for rapid legibility, and uniform UI elements.

  5. Collaborative Prototyping

    Agile UI Design: Divided the layout creation across the team (collaborating in pairs on specific screens) to build the final interactive, high-fidelity Figma prototype representing a seamless ambulance-to-hospital transition.
